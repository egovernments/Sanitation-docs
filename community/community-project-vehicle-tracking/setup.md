## Pre Configurations



### 1.0 Enable PostGIS Extension

PostGIS is an extension to PostgreSQL for storing and managing spatial information. To learn more about PostGIS, see [PostGIS.net](https://postgis.net/documentation/getting\_started/)

Tracking Service uses spacila data for tracking and storing geo coordinates and analysing the POI data. To make tracking service function properly, it requires support of the PostGIS extension on Postgres Database.&#x20;
